**Site:** src/__tests__/audit-coverage.test.ts:128, src/__tests__/audit-coverage-scan.ts:73-79.

**Bug:** Phase-3 placebo gate (R10-FG-4) at audit-coverage.test.ts:178-193 closes the `'individual' + tests:[]` loophole. It does NOT close the equivalent `'structural-gate'` loophole. The annotation cross-check at line 128 explicitly does `if (f.coverageStrategy === 'structural-gate') continue;`. Nothing in the gate suite verifies that:

1. A `'structural-gate'` entry's `tests[]` actually point at one of the source-scan-style structural fixtures (sibling-archetype-gate.test.ts, claim-archetype-gate.test.ts, audit-coverage.test.ts itself).
